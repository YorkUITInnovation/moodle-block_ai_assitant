## 🧠 **AI Course Assistant Block – Student User Guide**

### **Overview**
The **AI Course Assistant Block** is a centralized hub designed to support students throughout their course. It combines access to key materials with intelligent tools that personalize and enhance the learning experience.

---

### **📘 Syllabus**
- **Purpose:** Provides a downloadable version of the course syllabus.
- **Download:** Click the link to download or view the syllabus for reference throughout the term.
- **How to Use:** Click Agetn icon, usually at the bottom left of the page to starting chating with AL the Course Assistant.

---

### **🤖 My Learning Companion**
This section contains AI-powered tools to support your study journey:

#### 1. **Manage Saved Chats**
- **Function:** View and delete your previous interactions with the Learning Companion.
- **Interface:** Opens a pop-up titled **"Saved Chats"** displaying a list of saved entries.
- **Details Shown:**
    - **Chat Title** (e.g., *QuizMe ON...: Solar_Wind_OriginsThe_Parker_S*)
    - **Date & Time Saved** (e.g., *Sunday,
      3 August 2025, at 8: 53 PM*)
    - **Delete Option**: A trash can icon allows you to remove chats you no longer need..

#### 2. **My Tutor**
- **Function:** Access a personalized AI tutor for academic help.
- **Use Case:** Ask questions, get explanations, and receive guidance on course topics.

#### 3. **QuizMe ON...**
- **Function:** Take interactive quizzes on specific subjects.
- **Use Case:** Reinforce learning, test your understanding, and prepare for exams.

---

### **Tips for Students**
- Use **My Tutor** regularly to clarify doubts and deepen understanding.
- Save useful chats for future reference using **Manage Saved Chats**.
- Practice with **QuizMe ON...** to stay sharp and track your progress.
