### **Overview**
The **AI Course Assistant Block** is a centralized hub designed to support students throughout their course. It combines access to key materials with intelligent tools that personalize and enhance the learning experience.
Not all course material is available in the AI Course assistant. The AI Course Assistant is trained by your instructors based on course content your instructor selected to be trained.

> **Note:** If the AI Course Assistant block appears empty, it means your instructor hasn’t added any content yet or hasn’t enabled student access. Please check back later or contact your instructor for more details.

### **📘 Syllabus**
- **Purpose:** Provides a downloadable version of the course syllabus.
- **Download:** Click the link to download or view the syllabus for reference throughout the term.

#### **AI Course Assistant Agent**
- **Location:** Look for the AI Course Assistant icon at the bottom left corner of your course page
- **Icon Appearance:** The chatbot icon resembles Albert Einstein
- **How to Access:** Click the agent icon to open a chat pop-up window
- **Functionality:** 
  - Chat directly with the AI Course Assistant about course content
  - Ask questions about the syllabus and course materials
  - Get help with topics covered in instructor-trained course content
  - Access information from any materials your instructor has trained the AI on
- **Availability:** The agent is accessible from the course page for instant help

> **Note:** The AI Course Assistant is designed to assist with course-related questions and provide explanations. It does not replace human instructors but serves as a supplementary resource for your learning.

### **🤖 My Learning Companion**
This section contains AI-powered tools to support your study journey:

#### 1. **Manage Saved Chats**
- **Function:** View and delete your previous interactions with the Learning Companion.
- **Interface:** Opens a pop-up titled **"Saved Chats"** displaying a list of saved entries.
- **Details Shown:**
    - **Chat Title** (e.g., *QuizMe ON...: Solar_Wind_OriginsThe_Parker_S*)
    - **Date & Time Saved** (e.g., *Sunday,
      3 August 2025, at 8: 53 PM*)
    - **Delete Option**: A trash can icon allows you to remove chats you no longer need..

#### 2. **My Tutor**
- **Function:** Access a personalized AI tutor for academic help.
- **Use Case:** Ask questions, get explanations, and receive guidance on course topics.


#### 3. **QuizMe ON...**
- **Function:** Take interactive quizzes on specific subjects.
- **Use Case:** Reinforce learning, test your understanding, and prepare for exams.

#### 4. **Chat Interface Features**
When you select a subject and enter the chat window, you'll find the following features:

**Main Chat Area:**
- **Message Display:** Your conversation appears with your messages on the right (blue) and the AI tutor's responses on the left (white)
- **User Avatars:** Your profile picture appears next to your messages, and the AI assistant icon appears next to tutor responses
- **Scrollable History:** You can scroll up to review previous messages in the conversation

**Message Input:**
- **Text Input Field:** Type your questions or messages in the text box at the bottom
- **Send Button:** Click "Send" or press Enter to submit your message
- **Real-time Response:** The AI tutor responds immediately to your questions

**Chat Management Tools:**
- **Save Chat Button:** Preserve important conversations for future reference. Clickthe Save Chat button to download the chat to a PDF file
- **Summarize Chat Button:** Generate a summary of your current conversation in PDF format. Click the Summarize Chat button to create a concise overview of the discussion
- **Hamburger Menu (☰):** Access saved chats and additional options

**Saved Chats Menu:**
- **Access:** Click the hamburger menu icon (three horizontal lines) in the top-right corner
- **View Saved Chats:** See a dropdown list of all your previously saved conversations
- **Chat Details:** Each saved chat shows:
    - Chat title (subject and topic)
    - Date and time when saved
    - Delete option (trash icon) to remove unwanted chats
- **Load Previous Chats:** Click on any saved chat to continue that conversation
- **Delete Chats:** Use the trash icon to remove chats you no longer need

**Navigation:**
- **Help Text:** The blue information bar at the top provides guidance on using the chat
- **Responsive Design:** The interface adapts to different screen sizes for optimal viewing

> **Note:** The AI tutor is designed to assist with course-related questions and provide explanations. It does not replace human instructors but serves as a supplementary resource for your learning.

### **🔧 Troubleshooting**

#### **Understanding AI Limitations**
- **AI Can Make Mistakes:** Remember that AI assistants are powerful tools, but they are not perfect. Always verify important information with your instructor or course materials.
- **Double-Check Answers:** Use AI responses as a starting point for learning, but cross-reference with official course content when needed.
- **Ask for Clarification:** If an AI response seems unclear or incorrect, ask follow-up questions or rephrase your query.

#### **AI Course Assistant Agent Issues**

**If the Agent Times Out or Shows an Error:**
- **Restart Button:** Look for the "Restart" button at the bottom left of the text input box and click it to refresh the agent
- **Help Command:** Type `/help` in the chat to get assistance and troubleshooting tips directly within the agent
- **Refresh Page:** If problems persist, refresh your browser page and try accessing the agent again

#### **Chat Interface Problems**

**If the Chat Interface Doesn't Load or Shows Errors:**
1. **Return to Course Home:** Navigate back to your course's main page
2. **Access Manage Saved Chats:** Click on "Manage Saved Chats" in the My Learning Companion section
3. **Delete Problematic Chat:** Find and delete the chat that's causing issues using the trash icon
4. **Start Fresh:** Begin a new chat session by selecting your subject again

**General Tips:**
- **Clear Browser Cache:** Sometimes clearing your browser's cache and cookies can resolve loading issues
- **Check Internet Connection:** Ensure you have a stable internet connection
- **Try Different Browser:** If problems persist, try accessing the course from a different web browser
- **Contact Support:** If none of these solutions work, contact your instructor or technical support for assistance

#### **Best Practices for AI Interaction**
- **Be Specific:** Ask clear, specific questions to get better responses
- **Provide Context:** Give the AI enough background information about your question
- **Save Important Chats:** Use the save feature for valuable conversations you want to reference later
- **Regular Maintenance:** Periodically clean up your saved chats to keep your workspace organized
